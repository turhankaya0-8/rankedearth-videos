# Ranked Earth – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

**Ranked Earth** (Instagram: @rankedearth.8) publishes short educational videos. The "RankedEarth Publisher"
app is used only by the channel owner to upload these videos to the channel's own Instagram account.

## What data we collect
None. The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of Instagram users or anyone else.
It only uses the channel's own account to:
- read basic information about that account (username, number of posts), and
- publish the channel's own videos and captions.

## Third parties
Videos are temporarily hosted in this public GitHub repository so Instagram can fetch them for publishing.
They contain no personal data. Photos shown in videos are free-licensed images credited in each caption.

## Data deletion
Because no user data is collected, there is nothing to delete. For questions, open an issue in this repository:
https://github.com/turhankaya0-8/rankedearth-videos/issues

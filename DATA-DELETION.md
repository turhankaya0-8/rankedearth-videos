# Ranked Earth – Data Deletion Instructions

The "RankedEarth Publisher" app does not collect or store any personal data. It only publishes the
Ranked Earth channel's own videos to its own Instagram account (@rankedearth.8).

If you have interacted with the app and want any data removed, you can:
1. Remove the app from your account: Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites → remove "RankedEarth Publisher", or
2. Open an issue at https://github.com/turhankaya0-8/rankedearth-videos/issues and we will confirm deletion within 30 days.
